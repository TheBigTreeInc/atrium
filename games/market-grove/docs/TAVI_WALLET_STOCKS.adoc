= Market Grove — Tavi Wallet Gold exchange settlement contract
:revdate: 2026-11-13
:status: REQUIRED CROSS-PROJECT CONTRACT — NOT IMPLEMENTED IN TAVI WALLET

== Intended user-visible outcome

When the production integration is approved, Market Grove will use one spendable virtual currency: **Tavi Gold**. Users buy market instruments with eligible Gold and sell them to funded counterparties. A settled profit appears automatically in their authoritative home-realm Tavi Wallet and is spendable in the Tavi Assets Store. No cash-outs, creator payments, fiat or real-world compensation are supported. Positions cannot be sold outside their realm's approved game exchange.

Until this contract is implemented by the *owner*, Atrium 0.5.84 **remains a practice-only exchange**: `walletConnected:false`; `currencyId:practice-credit`; no trusted Gold transfer or market liquidation; never call a generic `wallet:reward` to mint sell proceeds. Game-only credits do not transfer into Wallet.

== Existing components and boundaries

* Tavi Wallet child `tavi-wallet` / `org.tavi.core.wallet` is authoritative for Gold issuance, restricted/free funds, entitled shopping-eligible funds, merchant purchases/refunds, journal, receipts and home-realm identity. Its currently reviewed service operations `wallet:merchant` and `wallet:reward` do **not** provide a multi-party stock exchange hold/capture/release/credit contract.
* Market Grove child `market-grove` owns only global simulated quotes, instrument inventory, exchange orders, matched trades and share register. Atrium catalog only discovers the game. TaviX is a packaging/launcher SDK and needs no new permissions for this exchange logic.
* Social verifies `(homeRealm, verifiedSubject)` and grants `tavi-wallet` typed cross-component authorization; Market Grove's standalone LoopRD API key is never itself an account proof. Browser JS cannot call privileged Wallet brokers or supply arbitrary account IDs.

== Wallet API extension required from owner

Propose `tavi.wallet-market-settlement/v1` as a **service-only** contract behind newly approved `wallet:market` scope, not a third-party TaviX capability. Every message binds `(marketId,homeRealm,verifiedSubject,orderId,settlementId,assetSymbol,amountGold,expiry,requestId)` to server-owned identities and approved price/quantity. The caller is a registered, operator-approved `market-grove` service only. Amounts are integer Gold and every call has an immutable idempotency key and tamper-resistant receipt.

. `hold`: reserve **shopping-eligible earned Gold only**, with bounded expiry and market order ID. Reject playOnly/free/refill Gold and self-grants.
. `commit`: transfer Gold between buyer's reservation and a *funded* seller or maker treasury with fee allocations, on an exact accepted fill. Do not issue currency. A trade cannot credit a seller before debit is accepted.
. `release`: return unfilled/canceled Gold to its original eligibility class; idempotent under retries.
. `status` and `reconcile`: query each durable wallet movement and unfinished prepared settlement after crashes/restarts; never guess from timeout.
. `positions`: read-only game-authenticated, subject-bound asset projection for the Wallet Investments tab (unrealized P/L is not spendable Gold).

Commit protocol: Market Grove persists a prepared matching plan and locks shares; Wallet durably holds and commits Gold under settlement ID; the game finalizes share ownership only when confirmed. A durable outbox/inbox and periodic recovery complete ambiguous or interrupted operations. Prevent race between cancel, partial fill, retry and expiration. No price oracle or UI-provided valuation can move Gold unapproved.

== Simulated trader funding rule

The 200 in-game bot accounts currently use independent practice-only balances to drive the simulation. In production mode each bot account must be mapped to an **approved allocation** within a pre-funded *Wallet Gold treasury*. No implicit migration or multiplication of seeded bot balances, no faucet spending, no debt. Maker orders must stop when wallet-backed Gold or inventory is exhausted; separate stabilization/rebalancing policy requires operator approval.

Protected 100 free/refill Gold is not tradable or launderable: users cannot deposit free Gold to bots, buy shares with it, exchange it for collateral or harvest sell payouts with it. The wallet should refuse market holds against non-shopping-eligible balances. Users who only have playOnly Gold can still enter/view the game, but require eligible Gold to invest.

== Implementation handoff and acceptance

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Owner |Required change
|`looprd-social` / `tavi-wallet` |Implement typed wallet market scope, Gold holds, settlement transfers, provenance, replay status, treasury funding, realm consent
|`atrium` / `market-grove` |Add cross-service prepare/commit/reconciliation adapter and share-lock state machine; keep current practice mode until end-to-end approved
|`tavi` client |First-party Wallet Investments read-only projection and Assets Store checkout of earned Gold
|`tavix` |No change for Market Grove hosted-web discovery or game-owned state
|===

Acceptance: two real Tavi users with different balances; buy/sell/partial/cancel/restart/service outage; conservation and idempotency across two databases; playOnly rejection; maker insolvency; malicious client amount/subject injection; market-account scope binding; optional player opt-in; Assets Store purchase funded by **actual settled sale proceeds**; ledger audit before any production grant or release.

No secrets, remote API origins, Wallet grant tokens or approval decisions belong in source ZIP, `atriumd.yaml` or static web assets. Do not publish a new Gold market before these gates pass.
